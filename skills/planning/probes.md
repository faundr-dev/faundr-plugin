# Probes by subject

Read only the section for the request's subject and take the 2 or 3 questions that change the result **in this** project. Skip what the code or the decisions already answer; if the project already has a way of doing it, the question becomes "keep what already exists?", with that as the recommended option. Always in terms of results, with options and the recommended one first.

## Login and account
- How does the person sign in: email and password, email link, Google, phone?
- Forgot password: does recovery need to be in this stage?
- Who can create an account: anyone or invite only?
- What happens when the person leaves or is gone for a long time (session expires)?

## Payment
- How do they pay: Pix, card, boleto? Once or monthly?
- What does the person get when they pay (email, access unlocked, invoice)?
- Payment that failed or was refunded: what changes for the person?
- Fixed price, by quantity or with a coupon?

## Notifications (email, push, WhatsApp)
- Who receives them and when: right away, once a day, only if the person wants?
- Through what: email, in-app notification, WhatsApp?
- Can the person turn the notification off?

## List, search and filters
- How many items does it usually have: dozens, hundreds, thousands?
- What shows when the list is empty (first time, search with no results)?
- Search by what and sort by what? Does it need filters?
- Show everything at once, in pages or loading as you scroll?

## File upload (photo, document)
- What type and up to what size?
- Who can see the file afterwards: only the uploader, the team, anyone with the link?
- Does it need cropping, compression or a preview?

## Metrics dashboard and reports
- What decision does the person make looking at this number? (without it, the dashboard is decoration)
- For what period: today, week, month, chosen by the person?
- Does it need downloading (spreadsheet, PDF) or sending to someone?

## Permissions and team
- Who can view, who can change and who can delete?
- Does one person see what belongs to the others?
- What happens to the data of someone who leaves the team?

## Personal data (LGPD)
- What personal data goes in (name, CPF, phone, address, health)? Is all of it needed?
- Does the person need to accept something first (terms, consent)?
- Can they ask for their data to be deleted?

## Integration with another service
- What happens if the other service goes down or is slow: the person waits, retries, gets a notice?
- Who pays for the service and is there a usage limit?
- Does data go back and forth right away or every so often?

## Calendar and times
- Time zone: is everyone in the same one?
- Can a booking overlap another time? Cancel and reschedule until when?
- Reminder before the time?

## Form
- What's required and what's optional?
- Does it save a draft if the person leaves halfway?
- What does the person see after submitting?

## AI inside the product
- What does the AI decide on its own and what does the person confirm first?
- When it gets it wrong, how does the person notice and fix it?
- How much can it cost per use, and what happens when it goes over the limit?

## Phone
- Will the person use it more on the phone or the computer?
- Does it need to work offline?
